Tour Overview

Experience the true pulse and grandeur of India’s commercial capital! The Mumbai City Highlights & Bollywood Glamour Tour is our signature 3-day handcrafted holiday designed to immerse you into the dual personality of the Maximum City: colonial Victorian heritage and modern Bollywood stardom.

From the historic arches of the Gateway of India to the iconic evening curves of the Queen’s Necklace at Marine Drive, explore UNESCO World Heritage architecture, ancient rock sculptures at Elephanta Caves, and enjoy behind-the-scenes access to live television and movie sets at Goregaon Film City.

Comprehensive Day-by-Day Itinerary

Day 1 South Mumbai Heritage Circuit

Colonial South Mumbai, Gateway of India & Marine Drive Sunset

Morning / Afternoon: Warm welcome upon your arrival at Mumbai Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj Airport or CST Railway Station. Our executive assists with private AC transfer to your selected 3★/4★ hotel. After check-in and brief refreshment, embark on a scenic heritage drive.

Sightseeing: Visit the magnificent Gateway of India facing the Arabian Sea, view the iconic Taj Mahal Palace Hotel, stroll through the vibrant lanes of Colaba Causeway, and photograph the grand Victorian-Gothic facade of CST Railway Terminus and Rajabai Clock Tower.

Evening: Conclude the evening with a relaxing stroll along Marine Drive (Queen’s Necklace), enjoying sea breezes and sunset street delicacies at Girgaum Chowpatty before an exquisite buffet dinner at your hotel.

Day 2 Elephanta Island & Film City

UNESCO Elephanta Caves & Bollywood Studio Sets Experience

Morning: Enjoy a wholesome breakfast at the hotel. Board a scenic catamaran ferry from Apollo Bunder over the Arabian Sea to Elephanta Island. Take the toy train and climb the ancient stone steps to explore the monumental 6th-century rock-cut Shiva sculptures, including the world-famous Trimurti.

Afternoon: Return to the mainland and drive to Goregaon for your pre-arranged Bollywood Film City Guided Tour. Experience real movie sets, sound stages, post-production labs, and witness dance routines or live television shoot sequences.

Late Afternoon: Scenic coastal drive over the Bandra-Worli Sea Link to Bandra Bandstand. View the celebrity mansions of Shah Rukh Khan (Mannat) and Salman Khan (Galaxy Apartments) with a seaside café halt.

Day 3 Divine Blessings & Departure

Siddhivinayak Temple Blessings, Shopping & Farewell Drop

Morning: Start your final day with spiritual blessings at the auspicious 200-year-old Shree Siddhivinayak Temple in Prabhadevi. Our chauffeur coordinates convenient drop-off and pickup.

Afternoon: Indulge in authentic Mumbai shopping for spices, textiles, and souvenirs at Crawford Market or chic fashion at Bandra Linking Road. Savour hot Mumbai street specialities like buttery Pav Bhaji, Vada Pav, and sweet Falooda.

Evening: Check-out and seamless transfer to Mumbai Airport or Railway Station with cherished memories of the Maximum City.
