Spiritual Journey Overview

Embark on Maharashtra's most revered divine pilgrimage circuit with utmost comfort and spiritual tranquility. The Mumbai, Shirdi Sai Darshan & Trimbakeshwar Yatra takes you to the holy abode of Sai Baba in Shirdi, the swayambhu shrine of Lord Shani Dev at doorless Shingnapur village, and one of the sacred 12 Jyotirlingas of Lord Shiva at Trimbakeshwar.

Designed specially for families and elders, this package ensures smooth highway driving in sanitized AC Innova/Crysta, verified vegetarian meals, comfortable accommodations near temple gates, and prompt assistance with online Darshan token reservations.

Day-by-Day Holy Schedule

Day 1 Mumbai to Shirdi (240 km / 5.5 hrs)

Mumbai Welcome & Scenic Drive via Kasara Ghats to Shirdi

Pickup from Mumbai Airport or Railway Station. Settle into your spacious AC Innova for a scenic drive over the misty Kasara Ghats to holy Shirdi. Check-in to your comfortable hotel situated close to the Sai Baba temple complex. In the evening, attend the divine Dhoop Aarti at Sai Baba Samadhi Mandir, visit Dwarkamai and Chavadi. Dinner at hotel.

Day 2 Shirdi & Shani Shingnapur

Early Morning Kakad Aarti & Shani Shingnapur Miracle Village

Early morning participation in Kakad Aarti and special Samadhi Darshan with prasadam. After breakfast, proceed on a 70 km excursion to Shani Shingnapur, the miraculous holy village where houses and shops have no locks or doors under the protection of Lord Shani Dev. Perform oil abhishek at the swayambhu black stone idol. Return to Shirdi for night stay.

Day 3 Shirdi to Nashik (90 km / 2 hrs)

Nashik Holy Circuit & Trimbakeshwar Jyotirlinga Darshan

Post breakfast, drive to Nashik. Proceed to sacred Trimbakeshwar at the foothills of Brahmagiri mountain, home to one of the 12 sacred Jyotirlingas containing faces of Brahma, Vishnu, and Rudra. In Nashik city, visit historic Panchavati, Sita Gufa, Kalaram Temple, and take holy holy water dip at Ramkund on the Godavari banks. Overnight in Nashik.

Day 4 Nashik to Mumbai (170 km / 4 hrs)

Muktidham Temple, Vineyards & Return to Mumbai

Morning visit to Muktidham marble temple and optional tasting stop at Sula Vineyards. Drive along the newly built Samruddhi Mahamarg back to Mumbai. Check-in to Mumbai hotel. Afternoon visit to Shree Siddhivinayak Temple for Lord Ganesha’s blessings, followed by an evening walk at Marine Drive. Overnight in Mumbai.

Day 5 Mumbai Sightseeing & Departure

Gateway of India Sights & Final Departure Transfer

Enjoy a relaxed breakfast. Quick tour of Gateway of India, Colaba shopping street, and drop-off at Mumbai Airport or CST Railway Station carrying divine blessings of Sai Baba and Lord Shiva.
