Grand Indian Heritage Circuit

The definitive Indian travel experience combining northern Mughal empire majesty and Rajasthani royalty with western India’s coastal metropolis! The Golden Triangle with Mumbai Express Tour seamlessly connects Delhi, Agra, Jaipur, and Mumbai in an 8-day luxurious odyssey.

Stand mesmerized before the pearlescent marble dome of the Taj Mahal at sunrise, ride to the hilltops of Amber Fort in Jaipur, explore Qutub Minar and India Gate in the nation's capital, and take a quick flight to Mumbai to experience the Arabian Sea breeze along Marine Drive and Bollywood glamour.

Itinerary Schedule

Day 1 Delhi Arrival

Welcome to Capital City Delhi & Monument Tour

Arrival in Delhi. Meet our chauffeur and transfer to 4★ hotel. Afternoon city tour covering India Gate, UNESCO Qutub Minar, Lotus Temple, and drive past Rashtrapati Bhavan. Overnight in Delhi.

Day 2 Delhi to Agra (210 km / 3.5 hrs)

Yamuna Expressway Drive, Agra Fort & Sunset at Mehtab Bagh

Drive via Yamuna Expressway to Agra. Check-in to hotel. Tour the red sandstone ramparts of UNESCO Agra Fort. In the evening, witness romantic sunset reflection of Taj Mahal across the Yamuna from Mehtab Bagh gardens. Overnight in Agra.

Day 3 Agra to Jaipur via Fatehpur Sikri (240 km)

Sunrise at Taj Mahal & En-route Fatehpur Sikri Ghost City

Early morning sunrise visit to the breathtaking Taj Mahal with private certified historian guide. Return for breakfast. Drive to Jaipur stopping at Mughal Emperor Akbar’s abandoned red stone capital Fatehpur Sikri and Buland Darwaza. Arrive in the Pink City Jaipur.

Day 4 Jaipur Sightseeing

Amber Fort, City Palace, Jantar Mantar & Hawa Mahal

Ascend to magnificent Amber Fort by jeep/elephant ride. Visit Sheesh Mahal (hall of mirrors), City Palace royal museum, UNESCO Jantar Mantar astronomical observatory, and photo halt before honeycombed Hawa Mahal (Palace of Winds).

Day 5 Flight to Mumbai (1 hr 45 min)

Flight to Mumbai & Marine Drive Sunset Promenade

Transfer to Jaipur Airport for your included domestic flight to Mumbai. Greeting in Mumbai and transfer to 4★ hotel. Evening stroll along Marine Drive (Queen's Necklace) with sea breezes.

Day 6 Mumbai Heritage & Elephanta

Gateway of India, Elephanta Rock Caves & Colaba Walk

Full-day South Mumbai heritage excursion: Gateway of India, ferry to Elephanta Caves island, Victoria Terminus (CST), and heritage walk through Fort & Kala Ghoda arts district.

Day 7 Mumbai Culture & Bollywood

Siddhivinayak Temple, Bandra Coast & Bollywood Tour

Visit Siddhivinayak Ganapati Temple, drive past Dhobi Ghat and across Bandra-Worli Sea Link to Bandra Bandstand and Juhu beach.

Day 8 Mumbai Departure

Farewell Drop at Mumbai International Airport

Breakfast at hotel and seamless transfer to Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj International Airport for your flight back home.
