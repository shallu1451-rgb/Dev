Coastal Expedition Overview

India’s ultimate sun, sand, and coastal lifestyle circuit! The Mumbai to Goa Sun & Sand Coastal Odyssey brings together the cosmopolitan glamour of Mumbai and the laid-back tropical paradise of Goa into one unforgettable 7-day holiday.

Begin with the historic colonial grandeur of South Mumbai, sea-facing sunset strolls at Marine Drive, and Bollywood vibes. Then take a swift flight south to the golden palm-fringed sands of Goa. Unwind in luxury beachside resorts, explore Portuguese colonial villas in Old Goa, sip feni cocktails at sunset shacks, and dance aboard an evening Mandovi River catamaran cruise.

Itinerary Schedule

Day 1 Mumbai Arrival

Mumbai Welcome & Marine Drive Sunset Stroll

Arrival at Mumbai Airport. Private transfer to your luxury hotel in South/Central Mumbai. Evening leisure walk at the Gateway of India, Colaba Causeway shopping, and sunset stroll along Marine Drive with street delicacies at Girgaum Chowpatty.

Day 2 Mumbai Full Day Sights

Elephanta Island, Siddhivinayak & Bandra Coast

Ferry excursion across Mumbai harbour to UNESCO Elephanta Caves. Afternoon visit to Shree Siddhivinayak Temple, Bandra-Worli Sea Link scenic drive, and evening at Bandra Bandstand viewing celebrity bungalows.

Day 3 Mumbai to Goa Flight (1 hr 10 min)

Flight to Tropical Goa & Beachfront Resort Check-in

Check-out and transfer to Mumbai Airport for your included flight to Goa Dabolim / Mopa. Warm welcome in Goa and transfer to your 4★ North Goa beach resort near Calangute / Candolim. Evening relax poolside or dip in the warm Arabian Sea waters.

Day 4 North Goa Exploration

Fort Aguada, Anjuna, Vagator & Vibrant Beach Shacks

Visit 17th-century Portuguese Fort Aguada and its historic lighthouse overlooking Sinquerim beach. Proceed to rocky Vagator Beach, the famous Chapora Fort ("Dil Chahta Hai" fort), and Anjuna Beach. Enjoy beach shacks with live music.

Day 5 South Goa Heritage & Cruise

Old Goa UNESCO Cathedrals & Mandovi River Sunset Cruise

Tour Old Goa’s iconic churches: Basilica of Bom Jesus (enshrining mortal remains of St. Francis Xavier) and Se Cathedral. Visit Mangueshi Temple and Panaji's colorful Latin Quarter Fontainhas. In the evening, board a 1-hour sunset cruise on Mandovi River with traditional Goan folk dances.

Day 6 Leisure & Water Sports

Free Day for Parasailing, Water Sports & Night Markets

Full day at leisure! Option to indulge in thrilling water sports at Baga/Calangute (parasailing, jet-skiing, banana rides), visit spice plantations with traditional lunch, or enjoy an Ayurvedic spa massage. Evening shopping at Goa night markets.

Day 7 Goa Departure

Farewell Goa & Outbound Journey Drop

Relaxed breakfast at the resort. Pack Goan cashews and Bebinca souvenirs. Timely chauffeur transfer to Goa Airport / Railway Station for your return trip.
