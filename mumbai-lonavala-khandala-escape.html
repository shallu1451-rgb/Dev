Hill Escape Overview

Trade the bustling concrete energy of Mumbai for the cool, mist-shrouded green hills of the Western Ghats! The Mumbai & Lonavala Khandala Scenic Hill Escape is our premier nature and leisure retreat combining iconic South Mumbai city sights with a tranquil stay in the Sahyadri mountains.

Cruise down the engineering marvel of the Mumbai-Pune Expressway, hike to Tiger's Leap and Lion's Point overlooking breathtaking deep gorges, splash in cascading hillside streams around Bhushi Dam, explore 2,000-year-old Buddhist rock monasteries at Karla and Bhaja Caves, and indulge in hot Masala Chai with freshly made crunchy Lonavala Chikki.

Itinerary Schedule

Day 1 Mumbai to Lonavala (85 km / 2 hrs)

Mumbai Welcome, South Mumbai Highlights & Hill Drive

Pickup from Mumbai Airport or Railway Station. Morning drive covering Gateway of India and Marine Drive promenade. Afternoon scenic transition onto the 6-lane Mumbai-Pune Expressway ascending into the Western Ghats to Lonavala (622 m altitude). Check-in to your cliff-side resort with valley views. Evening relax at the resort pool followed by dinner.

Day 2 Lonavala & Khandala Sightseeing

Tiger's Leap, Bhushi Dam, Lion's Point & Sunset View

After a scenic hill-view breakfast, drive to Tiger's Leap (Wagh Dari), a cliff with a sheer drop of over 650 meters resembling a leaping tiger. Visit the overflowing waters of Bhushi Dam, Lion's Point, and Khandala's Duke's Nose. Stop by famous artisanal shops to taste authentic Maganlal Lonavala Chikki and fudge. Dinner at resort.

Day 3 Karla Caves & Return to Mumbai

Ancient Karla & Bhaja Caves, Wax Museum & Mumbai Stay

Morning visit to Karla Caves, ancient 2nd-century BCE rock-cut Buddhist shrines containing the largest rock prayer hall (Chaitya griha) in India with intricately sculpted wooden pillars. Visit the celebrity Sunil's Celebrity Wax Museum. Late afternoon scenic downhill drive back to Mumbai. Check-in to Mumbai luxury hotel and evening free for shopping.

Day 4 Mumbai Departure

Siddhivinayak Temple Blessings & Farewell Drop

Enjoy a hearty breakfast. Visit Shree Siddhivinayak Temple and Bandra Bandstand before timely transfer to Mumbai Airport or Railway Station for your flight/train back home.
