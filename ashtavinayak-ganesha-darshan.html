Divine Yatra Overview

The Ashtavinayak Yatra ("Eight Ganeshas") is Maharashtra's most celebrated sacred pilgrimage. Unlike standard temples, each of these eight murtis is Swayambhu (self-originated rock manifestation), worshipped in peaceful rural valleys, hill caves, and river confluences across Pune, Raigad, and Ahmednagar districts.

Starting and concluding in Mumbai, Dev Tour and Travels ensures devotees travel with seamless comfort in clean private AC vehicles, stay at hygienic family-friendly hotels, and enjoy delicious satvik vegetarian meals throughout the 3-day spiritual odyssey.

Itinerary: 8 Ganesha Circuit

Day 1 Mumbai » Mahad » Pali » Pune

Varadvinayak (Mahad) & Ballaleshwar (Pali)

Early morning pickup from Mumbai. Drive towards Raigad district to visit Varadvinayak at Mahad, where an oil lamp (Nandadeep) has been burning continuously since 1892. Proceed to Ballaleshwar at Pali, the only Ganesha named after his devotee (Ballal) with a stone temple facing sunrise. Drive to Pune/Chakan for hotel check-in and pure vegetarian dinner.

Day 2 Theur » Siddhatek » Morgaon

Chintamani (Theur), Siddhivinayak (Siddhatek) & Mayureshwar (Morgaon)

Post breakfast, visit Chintamani at Theur situated at the confluence of Mula-Mutha rivers. Drive to Siddhivinayak at Siddhatek on the banks of Bhima river (where Lord Vishnu attained siddhi). In the afternoon, visit Mayureshwar at Morgaon, the foremost temple of the Ashtavinayak with a majestic peacock motif and Nandi facing the deity. Overnight in Pune circuit.

Day 3 Lenyadri » Ozar » Ranjangaon » Mumbai

Girijatmaj (Lenyadri Caves), Vighnahar (Ozar) & Mahaganpati (Ranjangaon)

Morning visit to Girijatmaj at Lenyadri, carved inside Buddhist rock cave No. 7 atop 307 stone steps (palanquin/doli service available for elderly). Proceed to Vighneshwar at Ozar with its shimmering golden dome, and conclude the yatra at the powerful Mahaganpati in Ranjangaon. Complete the circuit and return comfortably to Mumbai with divine blessings.
