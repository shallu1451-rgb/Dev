Royal Expedition Overview

Live like royalty across Rajasthan's most romantic and majestic citadel cities! Originating conveniently from Mumbai with direct domestic flights, the Royal Rajasthan Grand Expedition takes you across Udaipur (The City of Lakes), Jodhpur (The Blue Sun City), sacred Pushkar, and Jaipur (The Pink Capital).

Glide on sunset boat rides across Lake Pichola viewing Jag Mandir and the Lake Palace, admire the towering invincible walls of Mehrangarh Fort towering 400 feet above the indigo-tinted houses of Jodhpur, visit the world-famous marble Jain temple marvels at Ranakpur, and revel in authentic Rajasthani cultural dances and royal feasts.

Itinerary Schedule

Day 1 Mumbai to Udaipur Flight

Flight from Mumbai to Udaipur & Lake Pichola Boat Ride

Morning departure from Mumbai Airport on your included direct flight to Udaipur Maharana Pratap Airport. Warm traditional Rajasthani welcome and transfer to your 4★ heritage palace hotel. Afternoon tour of the grand City Palace complex overlooking the lake. Evening romantic sunset boat ride on Lake Pichola cruising past the white marble Taj Lake Palace and Jag Mandir Island.

Day 2 Udaipur City Sights

Saheliyon Ki Bari, Jagdish Temple & Vintage Car Collection

Explore the ornamental marble fountains of Saheliyon Ki Bari (Courtyard of the Maids of Honour), the 17th-century carved stone Jagdish Temple, and the royal Vintage Classic Car Museum featuring Rolls Royce and Cadillacs of the Maharanas of Mewar. Evening cultural folk dance performance at Bagore Ki Haveli.

Day 3 Udaipur to Jodhpur via Ranakpur (260 km)

Ranakpur Jain Sculptures & Arrival in the Blue City Jodhpur

Drive towards Jodhpur through the Aravali ranges. En-route stop at the stunning 15th-century Ranakpur Jain Temple, renowned for its 1,444 uniquely carved white marble pillars where no two pillars share the same design. Arrive in Jodhpur and check-in to heritage hotel.

Day 4 Jodhpur to Jaipur via Pushkar (330 km)

Mehrangarh Fort, Jaswant Thada & Sacred Pushkar Lake

Morning visit to colossal Mehrangarh Fort, one of India's largest forts featuring palatial courtyards and weapons galleries. Visit the white marble royal cenotaph Jaswant Thada. Proceed towards Jaipur, stopping at holy Pushkar to visit the rare Lord Brahma Temple and the sacred Pushkar Sarovar. Arrive in Jaipur.

Day 5 Jaipur Pink City Highlights

Amber Fort, Hawa Mahal, City Palace & Johari Bazaar

Morning jeep ascent to hilltop Amber Fort and Sheesh Mahal. Photo stop at Jal Mahal (Water Palace), visit the royal City Palace Museum and UNESCO Jantar Mantar observatory. Free evening for shopping for block-print textiles, blue pottery, and silver jewelry in Johari Bazaar.

Day 6 Forts & Rajasthani Feast

Nahargarh Fort Sunset & Chokhi Dhani Cultural Dinner

Visit Nahargarh Fort with breathtaking panoramic views over the Jaipur skyline. Evening visit to ethnic village resort Chokhi Dhani for traditional puppet shows, camel rides, folk dances, and an authentic royal Rajasthani banquet feast.

Day 7 Jaipur to Mumbai Return Flight

Return Flight to Mumbai

Enjoy royal breakfast at the hotel. Transfer to Jaipur International Airport for your included direct flight back to Mumbai, landing with unforgettable memories of regal Rajasthan.
