Mumbai Tour Packages Overview: 2 Days Express

Looking for top-rated, budget-friendly Mumbai tour packages without taking multiple days off? The 2 Days Express Mumbai Heritage Tour is meticulously planned for short vacationers, weekend explorers, corporate transit travelers, and families seeking the quintessential Maximum City experience.

As one of our most popular express Mumbai tour packages, this trip allows you to witness the architectural grandeur of the colonial era at the Gateway of India and Victoria Terminus, enjoy a breezy ferry across the Arabian Sea to the rock-cut Shiva shrines at Elephanta Island, and conclude with the sparkling evening sweep of Marine Drive.

Day-by-Day Itinerary for Mumbai Tour Packages

Day 1 South Mumbai & Coastal Sunset

Day 1: Colonial Heritage Trail & Queen's Necklace - Mumbai Tour Packages

Arrival & Check-in: Chauffeur greeting at Mumbai Airport or CST Railway Station. Transfer to your hand-picked city hotel for quick check-in.

City Heritage Trail: Visit the Gateway of India, photograph the regal Taj Mahal Palace Hotel, explore Prince of Wales Museum (Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj Vastu Sangrahalaya), and drive past the Flora Fountain and Bombay High Court.

Evening Delight: Head to Marine Drive for sunset. Enjoy authentic coastal evening snacks at Girgaum Chowpatty (Pani Puri, Sev Puri, and Kulfi) before a delicious dinner at your hotel.

Day 2 Elephanta Island & Departure

Day 2: Elephanta Caves Ferry & Departure - Mumbai Tour Packages

Morning: Savor a full buffet breakfast. Head to Apollo Bunder and board the 9:00 AM scenic tourist ferry across Mumbai harbour to Elephanta Caves.

Ancient Cave Exploration: Witness the 7-meter-high masterpiece Maheshmurti (three-headed Shiva) carved out of basalt stone. Browse local artisanal handicraft stalls on the island.

Afternoon Departure: Return to the mainland, enjoy a final authentic Mumbai street-food lunch, followed by chauffeur transfer to Airport / Station for your outbound journey.
