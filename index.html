Iconic Attractions

Must-Visit Places in Mumbai

Experience the vibrant pulse of Mumbai with landmarks spanning centuries of history, architectural brilliance, and coastal beauty.

Gateway of India

Built in 1924 overlooking the Arabian Sea, this grand basalt arch is Mumbai’s defining monument and starting point for Elephanta ferries.

Marine Drive & Chowpatty

Known as the "Queen's Necklace" for its sparkling evening streetlights. Perfect for serene sunset walks and savouring local street delicacies.

Elephanta Island Caves

A UNESCO World Heritage site featuring monumental 5th-century rock-cut Shiva sculptures located on an island an hour's ferry from Apollo Bunder.

Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj Terminus (CST)

A stunning Victorian-Gothic revival masterpiece built in 1887. An iconic UNESCO architectural wonder in the heart of Mumbai.

Shree Siddhivinayak Ganapati

Mumbai's most revered 200-year-old temple dedicated to Lord Ganesha, attracting celebrities, pilgrims, and dignitaries from across the world.

Bollywood Studios & Bandstand

Walk past legendary film star residences in Bandra, explore live film sets in Goregaon Film City, and enjoy sea breezes at Bandra Fort.
